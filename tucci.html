What Stanley
discovered with us.

Michele's Wines

Organic Cannonau, Rosé, Vermentino and Semidano — hand picked, organic, less added sulfites. Volcanic wines.

🍝

Lorighitas — Earring Pasta

Hand made in Morgongiori with Sardinian semolina from the Secci family.

🫙

Fregula Sarda

Hand made from the Marmilla. Toasted semolina pearls — ancient, nutty, impossible to find outside Sardinia.

🧀

Raw Milk Cheese

Goat and sheep's cheese made from raw milk, exactly as the shepherds have always made it.

🔥

Underground Suckling Pig

Suckling pig buried in the earth and slow-cooked for 12 hours with myrtle and aromatic herbs. The ancient Sardinian method. A dish you do not forget. It is extremely rare to find in Sardegna — a lost tradition.

🥛

Beelato — Sheep's Milk Soft Serve

Fresh sheep's milk, soft-served. Michele calls it Beelato. It stopped Stanley in his tracks. It stops everyone.

The Shepherd's Day · Fattoria Cuscusa

Come and
live the day
yourself.

We bring small groups to Fattoria Cuscusa for a full day with Michele. Walk his land, understand his animals, make cheese with Michele, cook together and sit at the same table. This is not a tour. It is an introduction to Sardinian agricultural, shepherding, cheese and wine-making.

The day includes
  • A full day at Fattoria Cuscusa with Michele Cuscusa
  • Farm walk — the sheep, the vines, the olive trees
  • Cheese making and tasting
  • Cooking ancestral Sardinian recipes together
  • Lunch at Michele's table — wine, cheese, bread, everything from the farm
  • Small groups only — maximum 8 guests
  • 12-hour underground suckling pig — must be reserved one month in advance. Not available in summer months.
